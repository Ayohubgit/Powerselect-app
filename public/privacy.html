Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains, plainly, what MyRateDial does with your data. It's a small, independently-run tool, and this policy describes exactly what happens — nothing more.

What we collect

  • Usage figures you type in (monthly kWh, seasonal breakdown, peak-hour share) — used only to calculate estimated costs, and not sent anywhere except to the calculation running in your own browser.
  • Plan details you enter for comparison (rates, fees, contract terms) — same as above, calculated locally in your browser.
  • ZIP codes, if you use the "Find plans in your area" search — sent to our server, which uses it to run a search via Anthropic's Claude API for electricity providers in that area.
  • Bill photos or PDFs, if you use the bill-scan feature — sent to our server and then to Anthropic's API to extract usage figures. A real utility bill often shows a name, address, and account number; be aware of that before uploading one, especially if you'd rather not share it. We do not ask the AI to extract or return your name, address, or account number — only usage numbers — but since the image itself is shared with a third party for processing, treat this the way you would any document upload to an AI tool.
  • Your IP address, temporarily — used only to apply a fair-use rate limit so one visitor can't use up the shared search budget. It's held in server memory, not written to a database, and is automatically cleared on a rolling basis (never kept longer than about an hour).

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email or name collection anywhere in the app.
  • No cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising identifiers.
  • No persistent database of anything you enter — usage numbers, plans, and bill uploads exist only for the length of your session in your browser.

Third parties involved

ZIP searches and bill scans are processed using Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models, and API request logs are automatically deleted on Anthropic's side after a short retention window (7 days, per Anthropic's standard terms at the time of writing). We don't control Anthropic's systems directly — this description reflects Anthropic's published terms as of this policy's last update, and you can review Anthropic's own privacy documentation at anthropic.com/legal/privacy for the current version.

How long we keep things

  • ZIP search results are cached on our server for up to 24 hours so repeat searches for the same area don't require a fresh lookup. This cache holds general market/plan information tied to a ZIP code, not to any individual person.
  • Bill photos are sent for processing and are not saved to any disk or database on our end — once your result comes back, the image isn't retained by our server.
  • IP addresses used for rate limiting are cleared automatically on a rolling basis and are never written to persistent storage.

Your rights

Because nothing you enter is stored in an account or database on our side, there is generally nothing to request access to or deletion of beyond the short-lived items described above. If you have any concern about data you've shared with the app — including a bill photo — you're welcome to reach out using the contact details below, and we'll look into it in good faith.

Where this data goes

Because Anthropic's infrastructure is used for ZIP search and bill scanning, relevant data may be processed on servers located in the United States, regardless of where you're accessing MyRateDial from.

Changes to this policy

If what this app collects or how it's processed changes — for example, if accounts, payments, or advertising are added in the future — this page will be updated to reflect that before the change goes live, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
